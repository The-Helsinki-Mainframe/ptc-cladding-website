Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 February 2026

Who we are

PTC Cladding operates ptcgr.com — a website for our facade and cladding installation services across the UK, Scandinavia, and the Baltics. Contact: info@ptcgr.com

What data we collect

We collect the following data only when you consent to analytics cookies:

  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • General location (country/city level — not precise)
  • Device type and browser
  • How you found us (search engine, direct link, etc.)

When you submit a contact, booking, or brochure request form, we collect: your name, email address, company name, and message. This is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site. This service sets cookies in your browser to collect anonymous usage data. No personally identifiable information is shared with Google via analytics.

You can opt out at any time by declining cookies in the banner or visiting Google's opt-out page.

How we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries submitted via our forms
  • To understand site usage and improve our content (analytics only, with consent)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Form submissions are retained for as long as necessary to fulfil your enquiry. Analytics data is retained for 14 months as per Google Analytics default settings.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@ptcgr.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The latest version will always be at ptcgr.com/privacy.
