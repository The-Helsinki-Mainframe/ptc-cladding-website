Who We Are

Built on Precision.
Proven at Scale.

Established in 2015, PTC Cladding has become a trusted partner in the European cladding industry, specialising in the installation of high-volume and technically complex facades and prefabricated structures.

We work closely with our clients to deliver projects that demand precision, expertise and an uncompromising commitment to quality — from the initial specification through to the final panel.

From a 10,000m² medical research facility in London to landmark residential towers across the UK and Scandinavia, our track record speaks for itself.

What We Do

Three Core Disciplines.
One Delivery Team.

Cladding Features

Installation of rainscreen claddings in a wide range of materials — GRC, aluminium & timber. Our services also cover balustrades, decking and other architectural features.

  • GRC & aluminium rainscreens
  • Timber cladding systems
  • Balustrades & decking
  • Architectural features

Prefabricated Structures

Large-scale facade delivery using prefabricated unitised aluminium-framed panels. Assembly of offsite-manufactured timber and precast structural or facade panels.

  • Unitised aluminium framed panels
  • Offsite timber panel assembly
  • Precast structural panels
  • Large-volume facade delivery

Glazed Elements

Installation of standardised and bespoke glazed structures. We work with aluminium and timber frame systems across a wide range of glazing applications.

  • Windows & doors
  • Curtain wall systems
  • Skylights & rooflights
  • Aluminium & timber frames
