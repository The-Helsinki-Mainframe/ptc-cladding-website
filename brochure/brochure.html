Who We Are

Built on Precision.
Proven at Scale.

Established in 2015, PTC Cladding has become a trusted partner in the European cladding industry, specialising in the installation of high-volume and technically complex facades and prefabricated structures.

We work closely with our clients to deliver projects that demand precision, expertise and an uncompromising commitment to quality — from the initial specification through to the final panel.

From a 10,000 m² medical research facility in London to landmark residential towers across the UK and Scandinavia, our track record speaks for itself.

What We Do

Three Core Disciplines.
One Delivery Team.

Cladding Features

Installation of rainscreen claddings in a wide range of materials — GRC, aluminium & timber. Our services also cover balustrades, decking and other architectural features, from straightforward repeating panels to bespoke signature elements.

GRC & Aluminium Rainscreens Timber Cladding Balustrades & Decking Architectural Features

Prefabricated Structures

Large-scale facade delivery using prefabricated unitised aluminium-framed panels. Assembly of offsite-manufactured timber and precast structural or facade panels — maximising programme efficiency and on-site quality control.

Unitised Aluminium Panels Offsite Timber Assembly Precast Structural Panels Large-Volume Delivery

Glazed Elements

Installation of standardised and bespoke glazed structures. We work with aluminium and timber frame systems across a wide range of glazing applications — from curtain wall systems and skylights to bespoke window and door packages.

Windows & Doors Curtain Wall Systems Skylights & Rooflights Aluminium & Timber Frames
